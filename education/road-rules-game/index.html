Australian Road Rules Game: Claude Fable 5.2 vs Gemini

Two browser games teach the road rules of New South Wales, Queensland and Victoria in English, Japanese, Korean, Simplified Chinese and Traditional Chinese. Each game was built by a different AI model from the same instructions. Both are learning aids, not official tests.

Last Verified: 10 November 2026 (phone numbers and the facts stated on this page)

Which game do you want to play?

Drive Down Under

Model: Claude Fable 5.2 (Anthropic, model ID claude-fable-5-2)

  • 3D engine: three.js (WebGL)
  • 28 questions, 5 languages
  • Each question links to the official page it was checked against on 10 November 2026
  • Where the official page does not answer, the game says "cannot determine"

Australia 3D Road Rules & 2026 Licence Test

Model: Google Gemini, Gemini desktop app, Task mode, model shown in the app as "4 Argon"

  • 3D engine: the model's own three-lite.js, drawn on a 2D canvas
  • 8 scenarios, 5 languages, plus a licence calculator
  • Not verified. Australia.md has not checked its questions, fines or demerit points against official sources

Disclaimer

These games are AI-generated learning aids. They are not official tests, not legal advice and not a substitute for the road rules of any state. Where do the two games disagree?

For a Taiwan driver licence in Victoria, the Gemini game states "FULL TESTING REQUIRED (Classified 'NON' by VicRoads)". The Claude Fable 5.2 game states "cannot determine". On 10 November 2026 the VicRoads conversion table coded Taiwan as EXP and the page did not explain what EXP currently means. Use the VicRoads licence conversion tool or phone 13 11 71.

No other content in the Gemini game has been compared with official sources.
