## Overview

**Barrack Heights** (postcode 2528) is a residential suburb in the City of Shellharbour local government area in the Illawarra region, approximately 16 km south of Wollongong CBD. Shellharbour City Council records that the Barrack Heights residential estate was established in the 1960s and adjoins Blackbutt Forest Reserve to the south. The 2021 Census recorded 6,003 residents in Barrack Heights. Current public sources confirm one in-suburb dental clinic operating from the Centre Health Complex on Captain Cook Drive.

All dental practitioners listed below are required to hold current registration with the **Dental Board of Australia** under the Health Practitioner Regulation National Law. Registration status for any individual practitioner can be verified at [Dental Board of Australia](https://www.dentalboard.gov.au) by clicking "Look up practitioners".
