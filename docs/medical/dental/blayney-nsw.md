## Overview

**Blayney** (postcode 2799) is a mixed residential and service town in the Blayney Shire local government area, about 35 km west of Bathurst in the Central West region of New South Wales. ABS 2021 Census QuickStats record 3,448 residents and 1,509 private dwellings in Blayney. Blayney Shire Council's offices are located on Adelaide Street in the town centre, and Transport for NSW lists Blayney Station on Railway Lane with commuter parking, wheelchair-accessible parking and toilets. Publicly available sources checked for this run confirmed one in-suburb dental clinic with a Blayney street address.

All dental practitioners listed below are required to hold current registration with the **Dental Board of Australia** under the Health Practitioner Regulation National Law. Registration status for any individual practitioner can be verified at [Dental Board of Australia](https://www.dentalboard.gov.au) by clicking "Look up practitioners".
