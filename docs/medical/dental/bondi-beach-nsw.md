## Overview

**Bondi Beach** (postcode 2026) is a mixed residential and commercial coastal suburb in Waverley Council, about 7 km east of the Sydney CBD. ABS 2021 Census QuickStats provides current demographic and housing data for Bondi Beach, and Healthdirect lists multiple dental services within the surrounding eastern suburbs catchment. Public directory checks for this run confirmed two in-suburb dental clinic listings with Bondi Beach street addresses.
