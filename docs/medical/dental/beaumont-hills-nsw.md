## Overview

**Beaumont Hills** (postcode 2155) is a residential suburb in The Hills Shire local government area, approximately 40 km north-west of the Sydney CBD. Australia Post lists Beaumont Hills as a 2155 delivery area. ABS 2021 Census QuickStats record 9,041 residents and a median weekly household income of $3,349 in Beaumont Hills. The Hills Shire Council maintains community facilities and reserves in Beaumont Hills including the Beaumont Hills Community Centre and Beaumont Drive Reserve. Publicly available sources for this run confirmed one in-suburb dental practice with a Beaumont Hills street address on The Parkway.

All dental practitioners listed below are required to hold current registration with the **Dental Board of Australia** under the Health Practitioner Regulation National Law. Registration status for any individual practitioner can be verified at [Dental Board of Australia](https://www.dentalboard.gov.au) by clicking "Look up practitioners".
