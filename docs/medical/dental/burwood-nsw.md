## Overview

**Burwood** (postcode 2134) is a mixed residential, commercial and light-industrial suburb in Sydney's inner west and the administrative centre of the Municipality of Burwood. The NSW Government's [Your Council NSW profile](https://www.yourcouncil.nsw.gov.au/council-data/burwood-1787123466/) places Burwood 12 km west of the Sydney CBD and describes its combination of residential, commercial and light-industrial development. The [Healthdirect Burwood dentist search](https://www.healthdirect.gov.au/australian-health-services/search/burwood-2134-nsw/dentists/310144008) returned multiple dental services with Burwood street addresses.

Registration status for any individual practitioner can be verified at [Dental Board of Australia](https://www.dentalboard.gov.au) by clicking "Look up practitioners".
