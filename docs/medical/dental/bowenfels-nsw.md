## Overview

**Bowenfels** (postcode 2790) is a mixed residential and health-services suburb in the City of Lithgow, approximately 145 km west-north-west of Sydney and 2 km west of Lithgow. Australia Post lists Bowenfels as a NSW 2790 delivery area, and the 2021 ABS Census recorded 2,050 residents in the suburb. Public health-service listings show part of the suburb's dental access is concentrated on the Lithgow health campus at Col Drewe Drive.

All dental practitioners listed below are required to hold current registration with the **Dental Board of Australia** under the Health Practitioner Regulation National Law. Registration status for any individual practitioner can be verified at [Dental Board of Australia](https://www.dentalboard.gov.au) by clicking "Look up practitioners".
