## Overview

**Caddens** (postcode 2747) is a residential suburb in the Penrith City local government area in western Sydney, approximately 53 km west of the Sydney CBD. Penrith City Council lists Caddens among the suburbs of its East Ward. The [2021 Australian Bureau of Statistics QuickStats profile](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL10741) recorded 3,479 residents and a median age of 33.

All dental practitioners listed below are required to hold current registration with the **Dental Board of Australia** under the Health Practitioner Regulation National Law. Registration status for any individual practitioner can be verified at [Dental Board of Australia](https://www.dentalboard.gov.au) by clicking "Look up practitioners".
