## Overview

**Belfield** (postcode 2191) is a mixed residential and local-commercial suburb in Sydney's south-west within the City of Canterbury-Bankstown local government area. ABS 2021 Census QuickStats records 6,555 residents in Belfield. Canterbury-Bankstown's 2024 Belfield Village Master Plan describes Belfield as a lower density residential suburb bisected by Punchbowl Road, about 13 km south-west of the Sydney CBD, with a village centre along Burwood Road that accommodates small businesses, cafes and restaurants. Publicly available sources checked for this run confirmed one in-suburb dental clinic with a Belfield street address.

All dental practitioners listed below are required to hold current registration with the **Dental Board of Australia** under the Health Practitioner Regulation National Law. Registration status for any individual practitioner can be verified at [Dental Board of Australia](https://www.dentalboard.gov.au) by clicking "Look up practitioners".
