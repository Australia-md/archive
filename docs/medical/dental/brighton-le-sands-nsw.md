## Overview

**Brighton-Le-Sands** (postcode 2216) is a mixed residential and commercial suburb in southern Sydney within Bayside Council. Public suburb references place it 13 km south-south-west of the Sydney CBD on the western shore of Botany Bay. Public suburb references also identify Lady Robinsons Beach and Cook Park along the suburb's eastern edge, while the 2021 Census recorded 8,336 residents. The Bay Street retail strip and surrounding foreshore give the suburb a concentrated local service area that supports in-suburb dental practices.

All dental practitioners listed below are required to hold current registration with the **Dental Board of Australia** under the Health Practitioner Regulation National Law. Registration status for any individual practitioner can be verified at [Dental Board of Australia](https://www.dentalboard.gov.au) by clicking "Look up practitioners".
