## Overview

**Braidwood** (postcode 2622) is a mixed residential and service town in the Southern Tablelands of New South Wales within Queanbeyan-Palerang Regional Council. ABS 2021 Census QuickStats report 1,412 residents in the Braidwood urban centre. Public suburb references place Braidwood about 91 km east of Canberra and identify it as a district service town on the Kings Highway for nearby tablelands and coast communities.

All dental practitioners listed below are required to hold current registration with the **Dental Board of Australia** under the Health Practitioner Regulation National Law. Registration status for any individual practitioner can be verified at [Dental Board of Australia](https://www.dentalboard.gov.au) by clicking "Look up practitioners".
