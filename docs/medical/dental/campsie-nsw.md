## Overview

**Campsie** (postcode 2194) is a suburb in the City of Canterbury Bankstown in Sydney's south-western suburbs. The City of Canterbury Bankstown identifies the Campsie Town Centre as one of its strategic centres and describes Campsie's setting by the Cooks River and Canterbury Hospital. [City of Canterbury Bankstown](https://www.cbcity.nsw.gov.au/your-council) · [Campsie Town Centre planning page](https://haveyoursay.cbcity.nsw.gov.au/campsie-masterplan)
