## Overview

**Batehaven** (postcode 2536) is a mixed residential and coastal suburb in the Eurobodalla Shire local government area, approximately 4 km south of Batemans Bay. The 2021 Census recorded 1,911 residents in Batehaven, with a median age of 54. NSW Government visitor information places Corrigans Beach Reserve opposite the Batehaven shops, reflecting the suburb's small local retail strip and beachside service role. Publicly available sources for this run confirmed one dental practice with a Batehaven street address within that local centre.

All dental practitioners listed below are required to hold current registration with the **Dental Board of Australia** under the Health Practitioner Regulation National Law. Registration status for any individual practitioner can be verified at [Dental Board of Australia](https://www.dentalboard.gov.au) by clicking "Look up practitioners".
