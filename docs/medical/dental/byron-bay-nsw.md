## Overview

**Byron Bay** (postcode 2481) is a coastal town in the Byron Shire Council local government area on the far north coast of New South Wales, approximately 165 km south of Brisbane. The [2021 Census QuickStats profile](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL10731) records 6,334 people in the Byron Bay suburb and a median age of 39. [Visit NSW](https://www.visitnsw.com/destinations/north-coast/byron-bay-area/byron-bay/hire/most-easterly-point) identifies Walgun Cape Byron, beside the town, as the eastern-most spot of the Australian mainland.

All dental practitioners listed below are required to hold current registration with the **Dental Board of Australia** under the Health Practitioner Regulation National Law. Registration status for any individual practitioner can be verified at [Dental Board of Australia](https://www.dentalboard.gov.au) by clicking "Look up practitioners".
