## Overview

**Bronte** (postcode 2024) is an established residential, beachside suburb in the Waverley Council local government area, approximately 8 km east of the Sydney CBD. Waverley Council describes the Bronte Precinct as bounded by Birrell Street, the Pacific Ocean, Bronte Gully and Brown Street. The [2021 ABS Census QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL10591) recorded 7,162 people in Bronte, with a median age of 38.

All dental practitioners listed below are required to hold current registration with the **Dental Board of Australia** under the Health Practitioner Regulation National Law. Registration status for any individual practitioner can be verified at [Dental Board of Australia](https://www.dentalboard.gov.au) by clicking "Look up practitioners".
