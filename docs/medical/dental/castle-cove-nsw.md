## Overview

**Castle Cove** (postcode 2069) is a residential suburb on Sydney's Lower North Shore, in the City of Willoughby, approximately 11 km north of the Sydney CBD ([Willoughby City Library fact sheet](https://libraries.willoughby.nsw.gov.au/files/sharedassets/public/v/1/ecm/willoughby-city-library-website/1-02castle_cove_1.pdf)). The 2021 Census QuickStats records a median age of 46 for Castle Cove ([Australian Bureau of Statistics](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL10844)); the verified practice is located on Deepwater Road within the suburb.
