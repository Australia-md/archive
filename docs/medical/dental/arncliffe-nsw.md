## Overview

**Arncliffe** (postcode 2205) is a mixed residential, commercial, and light industrial suburb in the Bayside Council local government area, approximately 11 km south of the Sydney CBD. Arncliffe sits south of the Cooks River and Wolli Creek and is close to Sydney Airport. The 2021 Census recorded a population of 12,023 people in Arncliffe. Local service activity is concentrated around Firth Street, Belmore Street, Forest Road, and Wollongong Road, which support neighbourhood healthcare and dental services.

All dental practitioners listed below are required to hold current registration with the **Dental Board of Australia** under the Health Practitioner Regulation National Law. Registration status for any individual practitioner can be verified at [Dental Board of Australia](https://www.dentalboard.gov.au) by clicking "Look up practitioners".
