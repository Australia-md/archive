## Overview

**Burraneer** (postcode 2230) is a bayside suburb in southern Sydney, approximately 26 km south of the Sydney CBD, in the [Sutherland Shire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sutherland_Shire) local government area. It sits on Burraneer Point on the north shore of the Port Hacking estuary, with Burraneer Bay to the west and Gunnamatta Bay to the east. [Australia Post](https://auspost.com.au/postcode/burraneer) lists 2230 as Burraneer's delivery postcode. The [Healthdirect service finder](https://www.healthdirect.gov.au/australian-health-services/search/burraneer-2230-nsw/dentists/310144008) confirms one dental service with a Burraneer street address.

All dental practitioners listed below are required to hold current registration with the **Dental Board of Australia** under the Health Practitioner Regulation National Law. Registration status for any individual practitioner can be verified at [Dental Board of Australia](https://www.dentalboard.gov.au) by clicking "Look up practitioners".
