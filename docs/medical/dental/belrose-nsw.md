## Overview

**Belrose** (postcode 2085) is a mixed residential and business suburb in Sydney's Northern Beaches Council area, in the Frenchs Forest Ward. Northern Beaches Council's profile for Belrose - Oxford Falls reports 8,860 usual residents and 3,161 dwellings in 2021, while public reference material describes Belrose as about 19 km north-east of the Sydney CBD and part of the Forest District. The suburb contains Glenrose Village and a larger business park precinct, giving residents local access to retail, services and health appointments within the suburb. Publicly available sources checked for this run confirmed one in-suburb dental clinic with a Belrose street address.

All dental practitioners listed below are required to hold current registration with the **Dental Board of Australia** under the Health Practitioner Regulation National Law. Registration status for any individual practitioner can be verified at [Dental Board of Australia](https://www.dentalboard.gov.au) by clicking "Look up practitioners".
