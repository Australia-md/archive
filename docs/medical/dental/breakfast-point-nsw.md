## Overview

**Breakfast Point** (postcode 2137) is a mixed waterfront residential suburb in the City of Canada Bay local government area on the southern shore of the Parramatta River. City of Canada Bay Council describes it as a waterfront estate with a small shopping village, while public suburb references place it about 16 km west of the Sydney CBD. Public suburb references also identify Breakfast Point as part of Sydney's Inner West and note that the suburb includes a local shopping centre where Canada Bay Dental operates.

All dental practitioners listed below are required to hold current registration with the **Dental Board of Australia** under the Health Practitioner Regulation National Law. Registration status for any individual practitioner can be verified at [Dental Board of Australia](https://www.dentalboard.gov.au) by clicking "Look up practitioners".
