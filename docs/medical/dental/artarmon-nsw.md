## Overview

**Artarmon** (postcode 2064) is a mixed residential, commercial, and industrial suburb in the City of Willoughby local government area, approximately 9 km north-west of the Sydney CBD. The 2021 Census recorded a population of 9,417 people in Artarmon. The suburb sits on Sydney's lower North Shore and has a concentrated village and service precinct around Hampden Road near Artarmon railway station. That local commercial strip supports the suburb's publicly listed dental practices.

All dental practitioners listed below are required to hold current registration with the **Dental Board of Australia** under the Health Practitioner Regulation National Law. Registration status for any individual practitioner can be verified at [Dental Board of Australia](https://www.dentalboard.gov.au) by clicking "Look up practitioners".
