## Overview

**Berry** (postcode 2535) is a mixed residential and tourism township in the City of Shoalhaven on the NSW South Coast, approximately 19 km north-east of Nowra CBD. ABS 2021 QuickStats record 3,098 residents and 1,232 occupied private dwellings in Berry. Berry's official visitor guide describes the town as an historic South Coast village with heritage buildings and rolling green hills, and Shoalhaven City Council describes Berry as a friendly historic town and major visitor destination. Publicly available sources checked for this run confirmed two in-suburb dental clinics with Berry street addresses.

All dental practitioners listed below are required to hold current registration with the **Dental Board of Australia** under the Health Practitioner Regulation National Law. Registration status for any individual practitioner can be verified at [Dental Board of Australia](https://www.dentalboard.gov.au) by clicking "Look up practitioners".
