## Overview

**Brunswick Heads** (postcode 2483) is a mixed residential and commercial coastal town in the Byron Shire local government area, approximately 17 km north of Byron Bay. It is located at the mouth of the Brunswick River on the far north coast of New South Wales. The 2024 estimated resident population for Brunswick Heads was 2,086, with a population density of 145.7 persons per square kilometre.

All dental practitioners listed below are required to hold current registration with the **Dental Board of Australia** under the Health Practitioner Regulation National Law. Registration status for any individual practitioner can be verified at [Dental Board of Australia](https://www.dentalboard.gov.au) by clicking "Look up practitioners".
