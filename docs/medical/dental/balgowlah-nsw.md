## Overview

**Balgowlah** (postcode 2093) is a mixed residential and commercial suburb in the Northern Beaches Council local government area, approximately 10 km north-east of the Sydney CBD. Australia Post lists Balgowlah as a 2093 delivery area, and the ABS-derived suburb dataset classifies it as a major urban locality in Greater Sydney. Publicly listed dental practices verified for this run are concentrated along the suburb's Sydney Road commercial corridor.

All dental practitioners listed below are required to hold current registration with the **Dental Board of Australia** under the Health Practitioner Regulation National Law. Registration status for any individual practitioner can be verified at [Dental Board of Australia](https://www.dentalboard.gov.au) by clicking "Look up practitioners".
