## Overview

**Berowra Heights** (postcode 2082) is a residential suburb in Hornsby Shire, approximately 39 km north of the Sydney CBD. ABS 2021 Census QuickStats record 5,286 residents and 1,867 private dwellings in Berowra Heights. Australia Post lists Berowra Heights as a delivery area for postcode 2082, and Healthdirect's suburb search returns two dental practices with Berowra Heights street addresses on Turner Road. This gives the suburb a small but verified in-suburb dental cluster serving general, restorative, orthodontic and emergency care needs.

All dental practitioners listed below are required to hold current registration with the **Dental Board of Australia** under the Health Practitioner Regulation National Law. Registration status for any individual practitioner can be verified at [Dental Board of Australia](https://www.dentalboard.gov.au) by clicking "Look up practitioners".
