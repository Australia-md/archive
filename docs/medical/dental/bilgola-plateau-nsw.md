## Overview

**Bilgola Plateau** (postcode 2107) is a residential coastal suburb in Sydney's Northern Beaches Council area, approximately 34 km north-east of the Sydney CBD. ABS 2021 Census QuickStats record 3,650 residents and a median age of 46 in Bilgola Plateau. Northern Beaches Council lists Bilgola Plateau within its Pittwater ward-area suburbs, and public locality references describe the suburb as a plateau settlement above Bilgola Beach with local shops, a primary school and access down to the coast. Publicly available sources checked for this run confirmed one in-suburb dental clinic with a Bilgola Plateau street address.

All dental practitioners listed below are required to hold current registration with the **Dental Board of Australia** under the Health Practitioner Regulation National Law. Registration status for any individual practitioner can be verified at [Dental Board of Australia](https://www.dentalboard.gov.au) by clicking "Look up practitioners".
