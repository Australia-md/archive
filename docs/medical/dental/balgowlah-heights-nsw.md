## Overview

**Balgowlah Heights** (postcode 2093) is a mixed residential suburb in the Northern Beaches Council local government area, approximately 11 km north-east of the Sydney CBD. Australia Post lists Balgowlah Heights as a 2093 delivery area, and publicly available suburb references place it on the Northern Beaches ridge above North Harbour. Publicly listed dental services verified for this run are concentrated at the suburb's Stockland Balgowlah commercial centre on Condamine Street.

All dental practitioners listed below are required to hold current registration with the **Dental Board of Australia** under the Health Practitioner Regulation National Law. Registration status for any individual practitioner can be verified at [Dental Board of Australia](https://www.dentalboard.gov.au) by clicking "Look up practitioners".
