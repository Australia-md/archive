## Overview

**Cabramatta West** (postcode 2166) is a residential suburb in the City of Fairfield local government area, approximately 32 km south-west of the Sydney CBD. It is part of Sydney's south-western region. The [2021 Australian Bureau of Statistics QuickStats profile](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL10739) identifies Vietnamese, Chinese, and Khmer among the leading ancestry responses reported in the suburb.

All dental practitioners listed below are required to hold current registration with the **Dental Board of Australia** under the Health Practitioner Regulation National Law. Registration status for any individual practitioner can be verified at [Dental Board of Australia](https://www.dentalboard.gov.au) by clicking "Look up practitioners".
