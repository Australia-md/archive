## Overview

**Berkeley Vale** (postcode 2261) is a mixed residential suburb in the Central Coast Council local government area on the NSW Central Coast. ABS 2021 Census QuickStats record 8,951 residents and 3,379 private dwellings in Berkeley Vale. The Lakes Family Dental states that its Berkeley Vale practice serves nearby Glenning Valley, Chittaway Bay, Killarney Vale, Tumbi Umbi, Bateau Bay and Tuggerah. Publicly available sources checked for this run confirmed two in-suburb dental clinics with Berkeley Vale street addresses.

All dental practitioners listed below are required to hold current registration with the **Dental Board of Australia** under the Health Practitioner Regulation National Law. Registration status for any individual practitioner can be verified at [Dental Board of Australia](https://www.dentalboard.gov.au) by clicking "Look up practitioners".
