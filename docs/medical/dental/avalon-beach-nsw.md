## Overview

**Avalon Beach** (postcode 2107) is a mixed residential and commercial beachside suburb in the Northern Beaches Council local government area, approximately 28 km north of the Sydney CBD. The suburb recorded a population of 10,379 at the 2021 census. Avalon Beach sits on Sydney's Northern Beaches peninsula between Newport, Whale Beach and the Tasman Sea. Its compact town centre along Avalon Parade and Old Barrenjoey Road supports publicly listed dental practices with verified Avalon Beach street addresses.

All dental practitioners listed below are required to hold current registration with the **Dental Board of Australia** under the Health Practitioner Regulation National Law. Registration status for any individual practitioner can be verified at [Dental Board of Australia](https://www.dentalboard.gov.au) by clicking "Look up practitioners".
