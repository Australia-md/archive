## Overview

**Bowral** (postcode 2576) is a mixed residential and regional service town in Wingecarribee Shire and the largest town in the Southern Highlands of New South Wales. Public suburb references place Bowral about 132 km south-west of Sydney and identify it as the main business and entertainment precinct of the Wingecarribee Shire. Australia Post lists Bowral as a NSW 2576 postcode, and Bowral's published historical population table on Wikipedia cites ABS census data showing 10,764 residents in 2021.

All dental practitioners listed below are required to hold current registration with the **Dental Board of Australia** under the Health Practitioner Regulation National Law. Registration status for any individual practitioner can be verified at [Dental Board of Australia](https://www.dentalboard.gov.au) by clicking "Look up practitioners".
