## Overview

**Busby** (postcode 2168) is a residential suburb in south-western Sydney in the City of Liverpool local government area, approximately 37 km south-west of the Sydney CBD. Liverpool City Council's local history record places the suburb's creation between 1961 and 1965 as part of the Green Valley Housing Estate.
