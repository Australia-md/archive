## Overview

**Annandale** (postcode 2038) is a mixed residential and commercial suburb in the Inner West Council local government area, approximately 5 km west of the Sydney CBD. Annandale forms part of Sydney's Inner West and sits between Glebe, Lilyfield, Leichhardt, Stanmore, and Camperdown. The 2021 Census recorded a population of 9,487 people in Annandale. Johnston Street functions as a local commercial spine, supporting neighbourhood services including dental practices.

All dental practitioners listed below are required to hold current registration with the **Dental Board of Australia** under the Health Practitioner Regulation National Law. Registration status for any individual practitioner can be verified at [Dental Board of Australia](https://www.dentalboard.gov.au) by clicking "Look up practitioners".
