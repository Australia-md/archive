## Overview

**Cameron Park** (postcode 2285) is a residential suburb in the City of Lake Macquarie local government area, approximately 16 km west of Newcastle's CBD ([Mapcarta](https://mapcarta.com/29042602); [Lake Mac Local History](https://history.lakemac.com.au/narrative/20)). Cameron Park Plaza is a local retail centre at the corner of George Booth Drive and Portland Drive; Pacific Smiles Dental is located there ([Cameron Park Plaza](https://www.cameronparkplaza.com.au/); [Healthdirect service profile](https://www.healthdirect.gov.au/australian-health-services/healthcare-service/cameron-park-2285-nsw/pacific-smiles-dental-cameron-park/dentists/0839523c-0f94-4835-951b-226c363ce973)).

All dental practitioners listed below are required to hold current registration with the **Dental Board of Australia** under the Health Practitioner Regulation National Law. Registration status for any individual practitioner can be verified at [Dental Board of Australia](https://www.dentalboard.gov.au) by clicking "Look up practitioners".
