## Overview

**Billinudgel** (postcode 2483) is a mixed village suburb in the Byron Shire Council local government area, approximately 23 km north of Byron Bay. ABS 2021 Census QuickStats record 261 residents in Billinudgel, and Byron Shire Council includes Billinudgel in its local suburb listings.
