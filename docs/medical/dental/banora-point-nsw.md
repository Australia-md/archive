## Overview

**Banora Point** (postcode 2486) is a mixed residential and commercial suburb in Tweed Shire, approximately 665 km north-north-east of the Sydney CBD. The 2021 Census recorded a population of 16,460 people in Banora Point. Australia Post lists Banora Point as a 2486 delivery area, and the suburb sits in the Northern Rivers region near the Queensland border with its eastern boundary aligned with the Tweed River. Verified dental services identified for this run are concentrated around Tierney Terrace and the Banora Point Shopping Village precinct on Leisure Drive.

All dental practitioners listed below are required to hold current registration with the **Dental Board of Australia** under the Health Practitioner Regulation National Law. Registration status for any individual practitioner can be verified at [Dental Board of Australia](https://www.dentalboard.gov.au) by clicking "Look up practitioners".
