## Overview

**Broadway** (postcode 2007) is a mixed commercial and urban locality in the City of Sydney local government area, adjoining the inner-Sydney suburbs of Ultimo and Chippendale. Broadway is a 700-metre road and urban locality running west from George Street towards Parramatta Road and City Road, close to Sydney's central business district. The Broadway Shopping Centre, UniLodge and nearby university and transport facilities form a major retail and service cluster, including several publicly listed dental practices.

All dental practitioners listed below are required to hold current registration with the **Dental Board of Australia** under the Health Practitioner Regulation National Law. Registration status for any individual practitioner can be verified at [Dental Board of Australia](https://www.dentalboard.gov.au) by clicking "Look up practitioners".
