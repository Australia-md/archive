## Overview

**Belmore** (postcode 2192) is a mixed residential and local-centre suburb in the City of Canterbury-Bankstown, approximately 11 km south-west of the Sydney CBD. City of Canterbury Bankstown's community profile says Belmore covers 2.77 square kilometres and had an estimated resident population of 14,435 in 2025, with population density of 5,202 persons per square kilometre. ABS 2021 Census QuickStats record 13,781 residents in Belmore, a median age of 38, and an average household size of 2.6 people. Publicly available sources checked for this run confirmed three in-suburb dental clinics with Belmore street addresses.

All dental practitioners listed below are required to hold current registration with the **Dental Board of Australia** under the Health Practitioner Regulation National Law. Registration status for any individual practitioner can be verified at [Dental Board of Australia](https://www.dentalboard.gov.au) by clicking "Look up practitioners".
