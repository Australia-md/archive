## Overview

**Castle Hill** (postcode 2154) is a mixed residential and commercial suburb in The Hills Shire Council area, in Sydney’s north-west, approximately 30 km north-west of the Sydney CBD. The 2021 Census recorded about 40,900 residents and a median age of 42 ([ABS QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL10846); [The Hills Shire community profile](https://profile.id.com.au/the-hills/about)). Castle Hill is served by Castle Hill Metro station and a town-centre retail and service area around Terminus Street; verified dental listings are also located along Old Northern Road and Showground Road.

All dental practitioners listed below are required to hold current registration with the **Dental Board of Australia** under the Health Practitioner Regulation National Law. Registration status for any individual practitioner can be verified at [Dental Board of Australia](https://www.dentalboard.gov.au) by clicking "Look up practitioners".
