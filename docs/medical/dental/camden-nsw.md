## Overview

**Camden** (postcode 2570) is a mixed-use town in southwestern Sydney within the Camden local government area, as shown on [Camden Council's suburb maps](https://www.camden.nsw.gov.au/community/community-information/suburb-maps). A [Camden Council town-centre paper](https://www.camden.nsw.gov.au/assets/pdfs/Major-Developments/Camden-Town-Centre-Urban-Design-Framework/Camden-Town-Centre-Opportunities-and-Issues-Paper-Final-April-2015.PDF) places Camden approximately 65 km southwest of the Sydney CBD; Council's [Visit Camden information](https://www.camden.nsw.gov.au/whats-on/visit-camden) notes road access via the M5 and M7. [Healthdirect's Camden directory](https://www.healthdirect.gov.au/australian-health-services/search/camden-2570-nsw/dentists/310144008) distinguishes Camden street addresses from nearby Narellan services; only practices with a verified Camden address are listed here.

All dental practitioners listed below are required to hold current registration with the **Dental Board of Australia** under the Health Practitioner Regulation National Law. Registration status for any individual practitioner can be verified at [Dental Board of Australia](https://www.dentalboard.gov.au) by clicking "Look up practitioners".
