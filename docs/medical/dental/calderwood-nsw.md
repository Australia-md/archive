## Overview

**Calderwood** (postcode 2527) is a suburb in the Shellharbour City local government area of New South Wales. Shellharbour City Council describes Calderwood as being on both sides of Calderwood Road, contained by Marshall Mount Creek and Johnston's Ridge, and home to one of the city's newest residential estates. Calderwood Family Dental is located in Calderwood Village Shopping Centre.

All dental practitioners listed below are required to hold current registration with the **Dental Board of Australia** under the Health Practitioner Regulation National Law. Registration status for any individual practitioner can be verified at [Dental Board of Australia](https://www.dentalboard.gov.au) by clicking "Look up practitioners".
