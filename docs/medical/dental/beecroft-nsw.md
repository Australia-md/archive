## Overview

**Beecroft** (postcode 2119) is a mixed residential and village-centre suburb in Sydney's Northern Sydney region, approximately 22 km north-west of the Sydney CBD. Australia Post lists Beecroft as a 2119 delivery area. ABS 2021 Census QuickStats record 10,291 residents in Beecroft, and public suburb references place the suburb within Hornsby Shire and the City of Parramatta. Transport for NSW lists Beecroft Station on the T9 Northern Line with commuter parking, bike facilities and wheelchair-accessible station amenities. Publicly available sources for this run confirmed seven in-suburb dental clinics or dental practices with Beecroft street addresses.

All dental practitioners listed below are required to hold current registration with the **Dental Board of Australia** under the Health Practitioner Regulation National Law. Registration status for any individual practitioner can be verified at [Dental Board of Australia](https://www.dentalboard.gov.au) by clicking "Look up practitioners".
