## Overview

**Broulee** (postcode 2537) is a coastal village on the New South Wales South Coast in the Eurobodalla Shire local government area, approximately 300 km south of the Sydney CBD. The 2021 Census recorded 1,945 residents in Broulee, with a median age of 50. Visit NSW describes Broulee Beach as a seven-kilometre beach and identifies Candlagan Creek and Broulee Island Reserve as local coastal features.

All dental practitioners listed below are required to hold current registration with the **Dental Board of Australia** under the Health Practitioner Regulation National Law. Registration status for any individual practitioner can be verified at [Dental Board of Australia](https://www.dentalboard.gov.au) by clicking "Look up practitioners".
