## Overview

**Broken Hill** (postcode 2880) is a mixed residential, commercial and industrial city in the Broken Hill City Council local government area in far western New South Wales. It lies on the Barrier Range near the South Australian border and is approximately 1,100 kilometres west of Sydney. Broken Hill City recorded 17,588 usual residents in the 2021 Census.

All dental practitioners listed below are required to hold current registration with the **Dental Board of Australia** under the Health Practitioner Regulation National Law. Registration status for any individual practitioner can be verified at [Dental Board of Australia](https://www.dentalboard.gov.au) by clicking "Look up practitioners".
