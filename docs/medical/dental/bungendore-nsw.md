## Overview

**Bungendore** (postcode 2621) is a mixed residential and commercial village in the Queanbeyan-Palerang Regional Council area of New South Wales. Queanbeyan-Palerang Regional Council's visitor information places Bungendore approximately 40 km east of Canberra and describes the village as a heritage and artisan destination with galleries, markets and local businesses. The locality is in the Southern Tablelands region and has a local dental practice serving residents of the town and surrounding district.

All dental practitioners listed below are required to hold current registration with the **Dental Board of Australia** under the Health Practitioner Regulation National Law. Registration status for any individual practitioner can be verified at [Dental Board of Australia](https://www.dentalboard.gov.au) by clicking "Look up practitioners".
