## Overview

**Cardiff** (postcode 2285) is a mixed residential and commercial suburb in the City of Lake Macquarie local government area, approximately 12 km southwest of the Newcastle CBD. [Lake Macquarie City Council identifies Cardiff as a key centre in North West Lake Macquarie](https://www.lakemac.com.au/files/assets/public/v/1/development/adopted_cardiff_strategic_planning_framework_9_august_2021.pdf) and describes its town centre as a business, live-work and transport hub. The directory below includes providers with verified street addresses within Cardiff; nearby suburbs are excluded.

Registration status for any individual practitioner can be verified at [Dental Board of Australia](https://www.dentalboard.gov.au) by clicking "Look up practitioners".
