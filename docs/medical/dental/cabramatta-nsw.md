## Overview

**Cabramatta** (postcode 2166) is a mixed residential and commercial suburb in south-west Sydney within the City of Fairfield local government area, approximately 30 km south-west of the Sydney CBD. Fairfield City Council identifies Cabramatta as a town centre with diverse dining, fresh food markets, specialty grocery stores, and cultural festivals. The [2021 Australian Bureau of Statistics QuickStats profile](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL10738) provides the suburb's demographic and household data.

All dental practitioners listed below are required to hold current registration with the **Dental Board of Australia** under the Health Practitioner Regulation National Law. Registration status for any individual practitioner can be verified at [Dental Board of Australia](https://www.dentalboard.gov.au) by clicking "Look up practitioners".
