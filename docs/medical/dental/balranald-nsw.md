## Overview

**Balranald** (postcode 2715) is a rural town in the Balranald Shire Council local government area, approximately 163 km east-southeast of the Mildura CBD. The 2021 Census recorded a population of 1,063 people in Balranald. The town sits where the Sturt Highway crosses the Murrumbidgee River in far south-western New South Wales. Publicly listed dental services in Balranald operate from the Balranald Multipurpose Service hospital grounds on Market Street.

All dental practitioners listed below are required to hold current registration with the **Dental Board of Australia** under the Health Practitioner Regulation National Law. Registration status for any individual practitioner can be verified at [Dental Board of Australia](https://www.dentalboard.gov.au) by clicking "Look up practitioners".
