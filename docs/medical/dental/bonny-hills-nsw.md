## Overview

**Bonny Hills** (postcode 2445) is a coastal residential suburb in the Port Macquarie-Hastings local government area on the NSW Mid North Coast. The ABS 2021 Census QuickStats page for Bonny Hills reports a population of 3,045 and a median age of 51. Port Macquarie-Hastings Council planning material describes Bonny Hills as a growing coastal village. Public source checks for this run confirmed one in-suburb dental clinic with a Bonny Hills street address and published contact details.

Registration status for any individual practitioner can be verified at [Dental Board of Australia](https://www.dentalboard.gov.au) by clicking "Look up practitioners".
