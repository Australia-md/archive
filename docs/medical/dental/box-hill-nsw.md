## Overview

**Box Hill** (postcode 2765) is a mixed residential growth suburb in The Hills Shire local government area in north-west Sydney, approximately 47 km north-west of the Sydney CBD. The Hills Shire Council identifies Box Hill as part of Sydney's North West Priority Growth Area, and the 2021 ABS Census QuickStats for Box Hill (NSW) report 6,443 residents with a median age of 31. Carmel Village is one of the suburb's active retail centres, and the checked public dental listings for this run identified one verified in-suburb clinic with a Box Hill street address.

All dental practitioners listed below are required to hold current registration with the **Dental Board of Australia** under the Health Practitioner Regulation National Law. Registration status for any individual practitioner can be verified at [Dental Board of Australia](https://www.dentalboard.gov.au) by clicking "Look up practitioners".
