## Overview

**Bexley North** (postcode 2207) is a predominantly residential suburb in southern Sydney, approximately 13 km south-west of the Sydney CBD. ABS 2021 Census QuickStats record 4,286 residents in Bexley North and a median age of 42. The suburb sits within Bayside Council and the St George area, with a small shopping strip around Bexley Road, Shaw Street, and Slade Road near Bexley North railway station. Publicly available sources checked for this run confirmed two in-suburb dental practices with Bexley North street addresses.

All dental practitioners listed below are required to hold current registration with the **Dental Board of Australia** under the Health Practitioner Regulation National Law. Registration status for any individual practitioner can be verified at [Dental Board of Australia](https://www.dentalboard.gov.au) by clicking "Look up practitioners".
