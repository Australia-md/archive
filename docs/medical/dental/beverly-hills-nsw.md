## Overview

**Beverly Hills** (postcode 2209) is a mixed residential and commercial suburb in southern Sydney, approximately 15 km south-west of the Sydney CBD. ABS 2021 Census QuickStats record 10,483 residents in Beverly Hills with a median age of 40. The suburb is split between Georges River Council and the City of Canterbury-Bankstown, and its main shopping strip runs along King Georges Road near Beverly Hills railway station. Publicly available sources checked for this run confirmed two in-suburb dental practices with Beverly Hills street addresses.

All dental practitioners listed below are required to hold current registration with the **Dental Board of Australia** under the Health Practitioner Regulation National Law. Registration status for any individual practitioner can be verified at [Dental Board of Australia](https://www.dentalboard.gov.au) by clicking "Look up practitioners".
