## Overview

**Bega** (postcode 2550) is a mixed commercial and residential town in the Bega Valley Shire local government area on the Sapphire Coast of south-eastern New South Wales. ABS 2021 Census QuickStats record 5,013 residents in Bega, and Bega Valley Shire Council describes Bega as the rural centre of the Sapphire Coast, set at the junction of the Bega and Brogo Rivers. Council visitor information places the wider Bega Valley about halfway between Sydney and Melbourne, while the Bega Valley Commemorative Civic Centre lists Sydney as approximately 430 km away by road. Publicly available sources for this run confirmed two in-suburb dental clinics or dental practices with Bega street addresses.

All dental practitioners listed below are required to hold current registration with the **Dental Board of Australia** under the Health Practitioner Regulation National Law. Registration status for any individual practitioner can be verified at [Dental Board of Australia](https://www.dentalboard.gov.au) by clicking "Look up practitioners".
