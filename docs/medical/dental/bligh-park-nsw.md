## Overview

**Bligh Park** (postcode 2756) is a residential suburb in the Hawkesbury City Council local government area, approximately 58 km north-west of the Sydney CBD. ABS 2021 Census QuickStats record 6,220 residents and 2,335 private dwellings in Bligh Park, with a median age of 32 and an average household size of 2.8 people. Hawkesbury City Council's suburb mapping places Bligh Park adjoining South Windsor and the Windsor Downs Nature Reserve, and current council facilities pages note the Bligh Park neighbourhood centre beside the local shopping centre. Publicly available sources checked for this run confirmed one in-suburb specialist orthodontic practice with a Bligh Park street address.

All dental practitioners listed below are required to hold current registration with the **Dental Board of Australia** under the Health Practitioner Regulation National Law. Registration status for any individual practitioner can be verified at [Dental Board of Australia](https://www.dentalboard.gov.au) by clicking "Look up practitioners".
