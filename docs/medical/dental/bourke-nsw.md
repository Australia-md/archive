## Overview

**Bourke** (postcode 2840) is a mixed service town in Bourke Shire on the south bank of the Darling River, approximately 778 km north-west of Sydney. Australia Post confirms Bourke as a NSW 2840 delivery area, and public town references identify it as the administrative centre of Bourke Shire. BACHS states it provides primary healthcare and dental services to Bourke and surrounding areas, giving the town a verified in-suburb dental access point from publicly listed sources.

All dental practitioners listed below are required to hold current registration with the **Dental Board of Australia** under the Health Practitioner Regulation National Law. Registration status for any individual practitioner can be verified at [Dental Board of Australia](https://www.dentalboard.gov.au) by clicking "Look up practitioners".
