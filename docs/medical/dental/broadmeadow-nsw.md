## Overview

**Broadmeadow** (postcode 2292) is a mixed residential and commercial suburb in the City of Newcastle local government area in the Hunter region of New South Wales. Public suburb references place it about 5 km west of the Newcastle CBD, and public suburb summaries describe it as the geographic centre of the Newcastle urban area. The suburb's main commercial hub is centred on Nineways, while City of Newcastle's Broadmeadow Place Strategy describes a broader precinct of about 313 hectares spanning major transport, entertainment and employment land. The 2021 Census recorded 1,688 residents, supporting a compact in-suburb dental service catchment.

All dental practitioners listed below are required to hold current registration with the **Dental Board of Australia** under the Health Practitioner Regulation National Law. Registration status for any individual practitioner can be verified at [Dental Board of Australia](https://www.dentalboard.gov.au) by clicking "Look up practitioners".
