## Overview

**Boomerang Beach** (postcode 2428) is a coastal suburb in the Pacific Palms precinct of the Barrington Coast within the MidCoast Council local government area, about 17 km south of Forster CBD. NSW Government and MidCoast Council sources describe Boomerang Beach as a popular surf beach with a sheltered curved shoreline and reserve facilities serving residents and visitors. Publicly available sources checked for this run confirmed one in-suburb dental clinic with a Boomerang Beach street address on Boomerang Drive.
