## Overview

**Berala** (postcode 2141) is a residential suburb in Cumberland City Council, approximately 17.7 km west of Sydney city. ABS 2021 Census QuickStats record 8,757 residents and 3,093 private dwellings in Berala. Cumberland heritage material says the suburb is bounded by Auburn, Lidcombe and Regents Park and lies in the Haslams Creek catchment. Publicly available sources checked for this run confirmed one in-suburb dental clinic with a Berala street address.

All dental practitioners listed below are required to hold current registration with the **Dental Board of Australia** under the Health Practitioner Regulation National Law. Registration status for any individual practitioner can be verified at [Dental Board of Australia](https://www.dentalboard.gov.au) by clicking "Look up practitioners".
