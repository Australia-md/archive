## Overview

**Bulahdelah** (postcode 2423) is a town and locality in the Mid North Coast region of New South Wales within the Mid-Coast Council local government area. The town is situated on the Myall River and is approximately 235 km north of Sydney. The former Pacific Highway route passed through the town before the highway bypass opened in 2013.

All dental practitioners listed below are required to hold current registration with the **Dental Board of Australia** under the Health Practitioner Regulation National Law. Registration status for any individual practitioner can be verified at [Dental Board of Australia](https://www.dentalboard.gov.au) by clicking "Look up practitioners".
