## Overview

**Cabarita Beach** (postcode 2488) is a coastal locality in Tweed Shire on the New South Wales North Coast, approximately 20 km south of Tweed Heads. The [2021 ABS Census QuickStats profile](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL10735) records 101 people in Cabarita Beach and a median age of 52. [Tweed Shire Council](https://www.tweed.nsw.gov.au/) identifies Cabarita Beach among the communities serviced by the Hastings Point wastewater treatment plant.

All dental practitioners listed below are required to hold current registration with the **Dental Board of Australia** under the Health Practitioner Regulation National Law. Registration status for any individual practitioner can be verified at [Dental Board of Australia](https://www.dentalboard.gov.au) by clicking "Look up practitioners".
