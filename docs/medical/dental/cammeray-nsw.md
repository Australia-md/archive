## Overview

Cammeray is in the North Sydney Council area of Sydney’s Lower North Shore. The [2021 ABS QuickStats profile](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL10777) records 7,088 residents and a median age of 40; [Australia Post](https://auspost.com.au/postcode/cammeray) identifies the suburb as postcode 2062. The verified in-suburb practices in this directory have addresses on Miller Street or Amherst Street.

All dental practitioners in Australia must hold current registration with the **Dental Board of Australia** under the Health Practitioner Regulation National Law. Registration status for individual practitioners can be checked at the [Dental Board of Australia](https://www.dentalboard.gov.au) by clicking “Look up practitioners”.
