## Overview

**Albury** (postcode 2640) is a mixed urban centre in the Albury City Council local government area, on the Murray River in the Riverina region of south-eastern New South Wales. It is a major regional manufacturing, retail, commercial, administrative and cultural centre. Albury-Wodonga lies approximately 300 km north-east of Melbourne, with the twin cities on opposite sides of the Murray River. ([Your Council NSW](https://www.yourcouncil.nsw.gov.au/council-data/albury-1787123466/); [Two Cities One Community](https://www.alburywodonga.gov.au/region))

All dental practitioners listed below are required to hold current registration with the **Dental Board of Australia** under the Health Practitioner Regulation National Law. Registration status for any individual practitioner can be verified at [Dental Board of Australia](https://www.dentalboard.gov.au) by clicking "Look up practitioners".
