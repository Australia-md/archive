## Overview

**Bradbury** (postcode 2560) is a mixed residential suburb in the City of Campbelltown local government area in south-west Sydney, approximately 50 km south-west of the Sydney CBD. ABS 2021 Census QuickStats record 9,433 residents in Bradbury, and Australia Post lists Bradbury as a 2560 delivery area. Campbelltown City Council's suburb history notes that Bradbury is named after early local landholder William Bradbury. Publicly available sources checked for this run confirmed one in-suburb dental practice with a Bradbury street address at the Bradbury Professional Centre.

All dental practitioners listed below are required to hold current registration with the **Dental Board of Australia** under the Health Practitioner Regulation National Law. Registration status for any individual practitioner can be verified at [Dental Board of Australia](https://www.dentalboard.gov.au) by clicking "Look up practitioners".
