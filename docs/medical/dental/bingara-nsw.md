## Overview

**Bingara** (postcode 2404) is a mixed rural service town in the Gwydir Shire local government area, approximately 150 km north of Tamworth in the New England region. ABS 2021 Census QuickStats record 1,318 residents and 732 private dwellings in Bingara. NSW School Infrastructure states Bingara Central School is situated on the Gwydir River in the New England region, and Gwydir Shire Council operates a Bingara office on Maitland Street as a local public service point. Publicly available sources checked for this run confirmed one in-suburb dental clinic with a Bingara street address.

All dental practitioners listed below are required to hold current registration with the **Dental Board of Australia** under the Health Practitioner Regulation National Law. Registration status for any individual practitioner can be verified at [Dental Board of Australia](https://www.dentalboard.gov.au) by clicking "Look up practitioners".
