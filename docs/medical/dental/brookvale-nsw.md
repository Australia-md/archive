## Overview

**Brookvale** (postcode 2100) is a mixed residential, commercial and industrial suburb in the Northern Beaches Council local government area, approximately 16 km north-east of the Sydney CBD. Northern Beaches Council describes Brookvale as a key strategic centre and the largest employment hub on the Northern Beaches, with a significant concentration of industrial land. The 2021 Census usual resident population of Brookvale was 3,496, according to the Northern Beaches community profile.

All dental practitioners listed below are required to hold current registration with the **Dental Board of Australia** under the Health Practitioner Regulation National Law. Registration status for any individual practitioner can be verified at [Dental Board of Australia](https://www.dentalboard.gov.au) by clicking "Look up practitioners".
