## Overview

**Bomaderry** (postcode 2541) is a mixed residential and town-centre suburb in Shoalhaven City Council, approximately 154 km south of Sydney CBD. ABS 2021 Census QuickStats record 6,738 residents and 3,185 private dwellings in Bomaderry. Shoalhaven City Council's community profile says Bomaderry is bounded by Meroo Meadow to the north, Bolong and Hanigans Lane to the east, and the Shoalhaven River to the south, while Shoalhaven DCP 2014 includes a dedicated Bomaderry Town Centre chapter for the suburb's commercial centre. Publicly available sources checked for this run confirmed two in-suburb dental services with Bomaderry street addresses.

All dental practitioners listed below are required to hold current registration with the **Dental Board of Australia** under the Health Practitioner Regulation National Law. Registration status for any individual practitioner can be verified at [Dental Board of Australia](https://www.dentalboard.gov.au) by clicking "Look up practitioners".
