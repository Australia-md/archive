# Domains

* [Geography & States of Australia](geography.md) - Australia occupies an area of 7,692,024 km², making it the sixth-largest country by total area and the largest country in Oceania.
* [Government & Constitution of Australia](government.md) - Australia is a constitutional monarchy and a federal parliamentary democracy.
* [Economy & Industry of Australia](economy.md) - Australia has a highly developed mixed-market economy.
* [Medical & Healthcare System of Australia](medical.md) - Australia operates a universal public healthcare system called Medicare, established under the Health Insurance Act 1973.
* [Education System of Australia](education.md) - Education in Australia is the joint responsibility of the federal government and state/territory governments.
* [Flora & Fauna of Australia](flora-fauna.md) - Australia is one of 17 megadiverse countries in the world.
* [History & Heritage of Australia](history.md) - Australia's national heritage places are listed in the Australian Heritage Database, administered by the Department of Climate Change, Energy, the Environment and Water.
* [Arts, Culture & Sport of Australia](culture.md) - Australian Broadcasting Corporation (ABC) — Australia's national public broadcaster, funded by the federal government but editorially independent.
* [Technology & AI Sector of Australia](technology.md) - Australia has a growing technology and innovation ecosystem.
* [Tourism & Travel in Australia](tourism.md) - Australia is consistently ranked among the world's top tourist destinations.
* [Indigenous Australia](indigenous.md) - Aboriginal Australians and Torres Strait Islander peoples are the traditional custodians of the Australian continent and its surrounding islands.
* [Environment & Climate of Australia](environment.md) - Australia spans multiple climate zones due to its vast size.
