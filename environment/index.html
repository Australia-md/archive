Climate Risks

Bushfire & Flood

Australia has the most fire-prone vegetation on Earth. The 2019–20 Black Summer burned 18.6M hectares. Cyclical flooding is linked to La Niña events.

Coral Bleaching

The Great Barrier Reef faces repeated mass bleaching events linked to marine heatwaves. Monitoring is led by GBRMPA.
