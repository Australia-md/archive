2. Audit Categories

2.1 Technical SEO

Score: 80/100

What Works

  • Robots.txt is comprehensively configured, explicitly welcoming all major search and AI crawlers (Googlebot, Bingbot, GPTBot, ClaudeBot, PerplexityBot, Applebot-Extended)
  • Canonical tags are 100% self-referential and consistently formatted with trailing slashes across all 97 pages

  • Fastly/GitHub Pages CDN delivers sub-10ms TTFB on cached requests
  • Mobile viewport meta tag is present, correct, and responsive on all pages

Findings

42 Published HTML Pages Missing from XML Sitemap High

42 production HTML pages are absent from sitemap.xml. While internal linking aids discovery, search engines rely heavily on sitemaps for crawl scheduling and canonical index prioritization of programmatic directory pages.

Recommendation: Regenerate sitemap.xml to include all 97 HTML routes with accurate lastmod timestamps (YYYY-MM-DD) and tiered priorities (1.0 home, 0.8 categories, 0.6 suburbs).

Raw Markdown (.md) URLs Declared in Standard XML Sitemap Medium

Standard web search engines (Google, Bing) expect sitemap URLs to serve indexable HTML documents. Mixing raw markdown documents into the primary sitemap dilutes crawl budget and generates non-canonical index warnings in Google Search Console.

Recommendation: Separate web sitemaps from AI manifests. Exclude raw .md files from sitemap.xml and serve them via llms.txt or a secondary ai-sitemap.xml.

Missing Security Headers (HSTS, CSP, X-Content-Type-Options) Medium

Direct hosting on GitHub Pages without a reverse proxy or Cloudflare CDN layer prevents injecting custom security headers, precluding HSTS domain preload eligibility.

Recommendation: Route DNS through Cloudflare (or a Cloudflare Worker / edge reverse proxy) to inject HSTS (max-age=31536000; includeSubDomains; preload), CSP, and nosniff headers.

Findings

Sub-optimal Title Tag Length on Top-Level Category Hubs Low

Titles under 30 characters underutilize Google SERP real estate and miss opportunities for secondary topical keywords.

Recommendation: Expand titles to 50-60 characters (e.g., 'Culture & Arts in Australia: Sovereign National Archive' and 'Flora & Fauna of Australia: Biodiversity & Species Records').

Heading Level Jump in Submit Page (h1 to h3) Low

Skipping heading levels breaks standard document outline semantics for screen readers and search engine crawlers.

Recommendation: Convert the <h3> headings in submit/index.html to <h2> to maintain strict h1 -> h2 -> h3 hierarchy.

2.4 Schema / Structured Data

Score: 93/100

  • Homepage implements comprehensive Organization, WebSite, and WebPage schemas with primary image and contact information
  • Directory pages implement MedicalWebPage, Dataset, FAQPage, and Dentist schemas with AHPRA clinic listings

Findings

Dentist Schema Missing GeoCoordinates, Opening Hours, and PriceRange Medium

Rich local business snippets and Google Maps integration require or strongly recommend geographical coordinates and operating hours for local pack eligibility.

Recommendation: Enrich the clinic schema generation pipeline to include geo coordinates (suburb centroid or clinic geocode), priceRange ('$$'), and openingHours.

Missing BreadcrumbList Schema on Sub-Category and Directory Pages Low

Without BreadcrumbList schema, search engines may display raw URL paths instead of formatted hierarchical breadcrumbs in search results.

Recommendation: Add JSON-LD BreadcrumbList schema to all sub-pages representing the visual trail (e.g., Home > Medical > Dental > Chatswood).
