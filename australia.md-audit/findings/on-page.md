## 2. Issues & Findings

### Finding ONPAGE-01: Sub-optimal Title Tag Length on Top-Level Category Hubs
- **Severity:** Low (-2 pts)
- **Confidence:** High
- **Evidence:** Two top-level category hub pages possess titles under 30 characters:
  - `culture/index.html` → `<title>Culture & Arts | Australia.md</title>` (29 characters)
  - `flora-fauna/index.html` → `<title>Flora & Fauna | Australia.md</title>` (28 characters)
- **Impact:** Short titles fail to utilize the available 55–60 character display window on Google Desktop and Mobile SERPs, forfeiting high-value contextual keywords.
- **Remediation:** Update the titles to target broader search intent:
  - `culture/index.html`: `Culture & Arts in Australia: Sovereign National Archive` (56 characters)
  - `flora-fauna/index.html`: `Flora & Fauna of Australia: Biodiversity & Species Records` (58 characters)

### Finding ONPAGE-02: Heading Level Jump in Submit Page
- **Severity:** Low (-2 pts)
- **Confidence:** High
- **Evidence:** In `submit/index.html`, the document structure jumps directly from an `<h1>` ("Contributing Data to Australia.md") to `<h3>Submit New Entry</h3>` without an intervening `<h2>`.
- **Impact:** Violates WCAG accessibility structure guidelines and breaks the semantic outline generated by search engine crawlers.
- **Remediation:** Change `<h3>` elements in `submit/index.html` to `<h2>` to preserve a clean `h1 → h2 → h3` heading progression.
