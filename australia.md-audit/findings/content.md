## 2. Issues & Findings

### Finding CONT-01: Top-Level Category Hub Pages Border on Thin Content (<250 words)
- **Severity:** Medium (-5 pts)
- **Confidence:** High
- **Evidence:** Analysis of body word count across all HTML pages identifies 8 category hub pages with less than 250 words:
  1. `submit/index.html` — 102 words
  2. `education/index.html` — 198 words
  3. `tourism/index.html` — 200 words
  4. `government/index.html` — 205 words
  5. `environment/index.html` — 223 words
  6. `economy/index.html` — 232 words
  7. `technology/index.html` — 232 words
  8. `history/index.html` — 240 words
- **Impact:** These pages primarily serve as navigation cards linking to deeper documentation files. Under Google's Helpful Content System and 2026 Core Updates, shallow navigation hubs risk classification as low-effort doorway pages if they do not provide standalone editorial value or direct answers to primary user queries.
- **Remediation:** Expand each hub page with 150–250 words of verified executive summaries:
  - Add official national baseline metrics (e.g. Australian Bureau of Statistics GDP contributions in `economy`, CSIRO biodiversity data in `environment`, total enrolled student statistics in `education`).
  - Introduce a structured "Direct Answer" summary box at the top of each hub suitable for search engine passage extraction.
