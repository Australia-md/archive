National Broadcasters

Australian Broadcasting Corporation (ABC) — Australia's national public broadcaster, funded by the federal government but editorially independent. It provides television, radio, and digital news across the continent.
URL: abc.net.au

Special Broadcasting Service (SBS) — A multilingual and multicultural public broadcaster reflecting Australia's diverse society.
URL: sbs.com.au

Film & Literature

Screen Australia is the national arts agency supporting Australian screen content. Notable filmmakers include Baz Luhrmann and Peter Weir, while authors like Tim Winton and Richard Flanagan define the literary landscape.

Major Events & Olympic Record

The Australian Open, held annually in Melbourne, is the first Grand Slam of the year. Australia has a storied Olympic history, hosting the games in 1956 (Melbourne) and 2000 (Sydney), and is set to host again in Brisbane 2032.
