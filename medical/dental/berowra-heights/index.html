Frequently Asked Questions

How do I verify a dentist's AHPRA registration in Berowra Heights?

All dental practitioners in Australia must hold current registration with the Dental Board of Australia. To verify any practitioner listed here, visit www.dentalboard.gov.au and click "Look up practitioners", then search by the practitioner's name. Registration status, conditions, and any disciplinary history are publicly searchable.
