Frequently Asked Questions

How do I verify a dentist's AHPRA registration in Barrack Heights?

All dental practitioners in Australia must hold current registration with the Dental Board of Australia. Visit www.dentalboard.gov.au and click "Look up practitioners" to search by practitioner name. Registration status, conditions, and disciplinary history are publicly searchable at no cost.

What dental services are available in Barrack Heights?

Shellharbour Dentists at the Centre Health Complex on Captain Cook Drive offers dental check-ups, dental cleans, dental implants, cosmetic dentistry, smile makeovers, dental veneers, crowns, bridges, teeth whitening, and emergency dentistry.

Which private health insurers are accepted at Shellharbour Dentists?
