How do I verify a dentist's AHPRA registration in Beaumont Hills?

Visit the Dental Board of Australia at www.dentalboard.gov.au and click "Look up practitioners". Enter the practitioner's name to check their current registration status, any conditions on their registration, and disciplinary history. All practising dentists in Australia must hold current registration under the Health Practitioner Regulation National Law.
