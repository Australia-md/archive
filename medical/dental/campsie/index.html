Frequently Asked Questions

How do I verify a dentist's AHPRA registration in Campsie?

All dental practitioners in Campsie must be registered with the Dental Board of Australia. You can verify any practitioner's current registration status directly via the Dental Board of Australia — click “Look up practitioners” and search by name.

Which dental clinics in Campsie are open on weekends?

Several Campsie clinics open on weekends: Dr W M Cheng Dental Surgery is open Saturdays (Mon–Sat 9am–6pm); Campsie Family Dental opens Saturdays and Sundays (Sat 9am–5pm, Sun 9am–2:30pm, with alternate Sundays 9am–5:30pm); and Campsie Dental Spa opens Saturdays 9am–4pm, though it is closed Sundays.

Is there a dental clinic near Campsie Station?

Campsie Family Dental is a five-minute walk from Campsie train station, and Campsie Dental Spa is a short walk from Campsie Station with free on-site parking noted by the clinic.

Which Campsie dental clinics offer emergency dental care?

Campsie Family Dental lists emergency dentistry among its stated services. No other clinic profile in Campsie explicitly states emergency dental care at time of verification.
