Which Macquarie Park dental clinics open in the evening?

Where can I get specialist endodontic care in Macquarie Park?

Macquarie Park Endodontics (Hobart Building, HG01/2 Mooltan Avenue) provides specialist endodontic care and root canal treatment. Phone (02) 9449 9568.

Which Macquarie Park dental clinics work with health funds?

Bupa Dental — Macquarie Centre is a Bupa Australia preferred provider and a Members First Ultimate practice. MHP Dental is a preferred provider for Medibank Private and Bupa. Macquarie Dentists is a CBHS Dental Choice Network provider. North Ryde Dentistry — Macquarie Park has HICAPS on site and is a Westfund Provider of Choice. MacPark Dental accepts major health funds with on-site HICAPS claims. Check your own fund's terms with the clinic.

How do I verify a dentist's AHPRA registration in Macquarie Park?

Search the public register at the Dental Board of Australia (www.dentalboard.gov.au) by clicking “Look up practitioners”, or use the AHPRA Practitioner Register. The register shows each practitioner's registration status, conditions and any disciplinary history. Practitioners at the clinics listed here were not individually matched against the register at this check, so confirm each dentist before booking.
