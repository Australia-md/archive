Frequently Asked Questions

How do I verify a dentist's AHPRA registration in Balmain?

All dental practitioners in Australia must hold current registration with the Dental Board of Australia. You can verify any practitioner's registration status, conditions, and disciplinary history by visiting www.dentalboard.gov.au and clicking "Look up practitioners". Search by the practitioner's name to confirm their current registration.
