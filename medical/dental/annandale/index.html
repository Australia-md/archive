Annandale (postcode 2038) is a mixed residential and commercial suburb in the Inner West Council local government area, approximately 5 km west of the Sydney CBD, between Glebe, Lilyfield, Leichhardt, Stanmore, and Camperdown. The 2021 Census recorded a population of 9,487. Johnston Street functions as the local commercial spine and supports neighbourhood services including dental practices. The 2 dental practices below operate under the registration framework of the Dental Board of Australia under AHPRA. Last verified: 27 April 2026.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I verify a dentist’s AHPRA registration in Annandale?

Use the Dental Board of Australia register at dentalboard.gov.au and click “Look up practitioners”, or search the AHPRA practitioner register at practitioners.ahpra.gov.au. Both are free public registers maintained under the Health Practitioner Regulation National Law.

Which Annandale dental clinics are open on weekends?

Annandale Dental at 4 Johnston Street lists Saturday hours of 9am–1pm on alternate Saturdays, and is closed on Sundays. Saturday and Sunday hours for Teeth Plus Clinic at 79A Nelson Street were not confirmed from public sources at the time of verification — contact the clinic directly to confirm weekend availability.

Which Annandale dental clinics offer Invisalign?

Both Annandale Dental and Teeth Plus Clinic list Invisalign among their services according to the public clinic profiles cited on this page. Suitability for Invisalign treatment is determined by an in-person clinical assessment.

Which Annandale dental clinics offer emergency dental care?

Teeth Plus Clinic at 79A Nelson Street lists emergency dentistry among its services. Emergency dental care is not listed in the public profile of Annandale Dental at time of verification.
