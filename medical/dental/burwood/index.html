Frequently Asked Questions

How do I verify a dentist's AHPRA registration in Burwood?

All dental practitioners in Burwood must hold current registration with the Dental Board of Australia. Search the public register at dentalboard.gov.au by selecting "Look up practitioners".

Do Burwood dental clinics require an appointment?

Policies vary by clinic. Most Burwood dental practices listed here require an appointment, while a smaller number accept walk-ins and ask that you call ahead to confirm availability. Check the individual listing above for each clinic's access policy.

Are there Cantonese or Mandarin-speaking dentists in Burwood?

Yes. The Healthdirect practice profile for Dr Kenneth Wong, who practises at Dental 266 and Wong and Kao Dental Surgery on Burwood Road, notes that he speaks Cantonese and Mandarin. Confirm current language capabilities directly with the practice.
