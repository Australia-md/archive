Albion Park (postcode 2527) is a mixed residential and commercial suburb in the Shellharbour City Council local government area, approximately 23 km south of Wollongong. Shellharbour City Council describes it as a suburb on the Illawarra Highway, bounded broadly by Macquarie Rivulet, Frazers Creek and Hazelton Creek. Frequently Asked Questions

How do I verify a dentist's AHPRA registration in Albion Park?

Visit the Dental Board of Australia at www.dentalboard.gov.au, click "Look up practitioners" and search by practitioner name. Registration status, conditions and disciplinary history are publicly searchable.

Which dental clinics in Albion Park open on Saturdays?

Albion Park Dental Surgery lists Saturday hours of 9am–4pm. Tongarra Road Family Dental lists Saturday as closed. Confirm hours directly with the clinic before visiting.

Which Albion Park clinics list emergency dental care?

Albion Park Dental Surgery lists emergency treatment. Tongarra Road Family Dental lists emergency dental care and same-day emergency appointments when available.

Which health funds does Tongarra Road Family Dental list as preferred providers?

Tongarra Road Family Dental lists HCF, CBHS, Westfund and nib as preferred providers, and states that it also accepts other health funds.

Where can I park at Tongarra Road Family Dental?

Two dedicated parking spaces marked G01 sit behind the building. Additional parking is available behind the Albion Park Service Centre arcade via McGrath Lane, and free one-hour parking is available along Tongarra Road.
