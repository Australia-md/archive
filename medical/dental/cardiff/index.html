Frequently Asked Questions

How many dental clinics are listed in Cardiff NSW 2285?

3 dental clinics are listed in Cardiff NSW 2285: Cardiff Dental (30 Veronica Street), MS Dental Cardiff (20 Newcastle Street) and Word of Mouth Denture Clinic (29A Veronica Street).

Which dental clinics in Cardiff are open on Saturdays?

One clinic lists Saturday opening hours: MS Dental Cardiff, 9:00am–4:00pm. Word of Mouth Denture Clinic offers Saturday morning appointments by arrangement. Cardiff Dental is closed on Saturday and Sunday.

Which Cardiff clinics offer dental implants or orthodontics?

Cardiff Dental and MS Dental Cardiff both list dental implants and orthodontics.

Where can I get dentures in Cardiff?

Word of Mouth Denture Clinic at 29A Veronica Street lists full and partial dentures, denture repairs, relines and adjustments, and mouthguards. MS Dental Cardiff also lists dentures.

Which Cardiff clinics list emergency dentistry?

Cardiff Dental ((02) 4954 6888) and MS Dental Cardiff ((02) 4954 7722) both list emergency dentistry. Phone the clinic directly to check availability.

Which health funds do Cardiff dental clinics work with?

Cardiff Dental is a preferred provider for nib and HCF. MS Dental Cardiff has preferred-provider arrangements with HCF, Westfund, CBHS and SMILE, and accepts other major funds. Check your cover with your fund and the clinic before treatment.

How do I verify a dentist's AHPRA registration in Cardiff?

Search the national register on the Dental Board of Australia website by clicking "Look up practitioners", or use the AHPRA Register of Practitioners. The register shows each practitioner's registration status, conditions and disciplinary history.
