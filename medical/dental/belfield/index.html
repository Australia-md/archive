Frequently Asked Questions

How do I verify a dentist's AHPRA registration in Belfield?

Visit the Dental Board of Australia at www.dentalboard.gov.au and click "Look up practitioners" to search by practitioner name. This publicly accessible register shows current registration status, conditions, and disciplinary history for all registered dental practitioners in Australia.

What services does Belfield Dental offer?

Belfield Dental (Shop 3 / 37–39 Burwood Road) offers a broad range of services including comprehensive evaluation, preventative dentistry, teeth whitening, aesthetic dentistry, root canal treatment, dental veneers, crowns and bridges, dental implants, wisdom teeth removal, gum treatment, children's dentistry, and dental emergencies.

What are the opening hours for Belfield Dental?

Belfield Dental is open Monday, Tuesday, Thursday, and Friday 9:00am–5:00pm, and Saturday 9:00am–1:00pm. The clinic is closed on Wednesday and Sunday. Always confirm hours directly with the clinic before attending.
