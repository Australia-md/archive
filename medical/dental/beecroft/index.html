Frequently Asked Questions

How do I verify a dentist's AHPRA registration in Beecroft?

Visit the Dental Board of Australia at www.dentalboard.gov.au and click "Look up practitioners". Enter the dentist's name to view their current registration status, conditions, and disciplinary history. All dental practitioners in Australia must hold current registration under the Health Practitioner Regulation National Law.

Which dental clinics in Beecroft are open on weekends?

Beecroft Elegant Dental Clinic (18-24 Hannah Street) is open Monday to Saturday 7:30am–10:00pm and Sunday 9:00am–10:00pm. Beecroft Smiles Dental Surgery (7 Wongala Crescent) is open Saturday 9:00am–4:00pm. McConnell Dental (6-8 Hannah Street) is open Saturday 8:00am–12:00pm. Dentistry For Life (12-14 Hannah Street) is open Saturday 8:30am–1:00pm. Orchard Dental (73 Orchard Road) is open alternate Saturdays 9:00am–1:30pm.

Which Beecroft dental clinics offer emergency dental care?

McConnell Dental (Suite 36, 6-8 Hannah Street) lists emergency dental care as a service. Dentistry For Life (12-14 Hannah Street) lists emergency treatment among its services. Orchard Dental (73 Orchard Road) lists emergency dentistry treatments and sleep dentistry on request. Contact clinics directly to confirm current availability.

Is there a dental clinic near Beecroft train station?

Dentistry For Life at 12-14 Hannah Street, Beecroft states it is approximately a minute's walk from Beecroft Station on the T9 Northern Line.
