Frequently Asked Questions

How do I verify a dentist's AHPRA registration in Belrose?

Visit the Dental Board of Australia at www.dentalboard.gov.au and click "Look up practitioners". Search by the practitioner's name to confirm current registration status, any conditions, and disciplinary history. This register is publicly accessible at no cost.

Which dental clinics in Belrose are open on weekends?

Pacific Smiles Dental - Belrose is open on Saturdays and Sundays. The clinic's own website lists Saturday 9:00am–5:00pm and Sunday 9:00am–5:00pm, though Healthdirect records Sunday hours as 10:00am–4:00pm. Call (02) 8044 6444 to confirm current trading hours before visiting.

Which Belrose dental clinics offer emergency dental care?

Pacific Smiles Dental - Belrose lists emergency dental care among its services. Contact the clinic directly on (02) 8044 6444 to confirm availability and whether an appointment is required.

Is there a dental clinic near Glenrose Village in Belrose?
