Frequently Asked Questions

How do I verify a dentist's AHPRA registration in Bankstown?

Visit the Dental Board of Australia at www.dentalboard.gov.au and click "Look up practitioners" to search by practitioner name. This register is publicly accessible and shows current registration status, any conditions, and disciplinary history for all dental practitioners in Australia including those in Bankstown.

Which dental clinics in Bankstown are open on Sundays?

Three verified clinics in Bankstown are open on Sundays: Perfection Dental (Mon–Sun 9:30am–11:00pm), Always Smiles Dental (Mon–Sun 9:00am–5:00pm, public holidays closed), and Pacific Smiles Dental Bankstown (Sun 10:00am–4:00pm). Always confirm current hours directly with each clinic before visiting.

Is there a dental clinic inside Bankstown Central shopping centre?

Yes. Pacific Smiles Dental Bankstown is located at Bankstown Central, 1 North Terrace, Bankstown NSW 2200. The shopping centre offers more than 3,000 car spaces and is accessible via public transport. Contact the clinic on (02) 9205 4000 for appointment bookings.

Which dental clinics in Bankstown accept Bupa or Medibank?

Looks Wow Dental Clinic lists Bupa, Medibank, HCF, and other major health funds. Smile Dental Bankstown lists Bupa, Health Partners, Medibank, and Teachers Health. Always confirm current fund arrangements directly with the clinic, as health fund participation can change.
