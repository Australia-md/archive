How do I verify a dentist's AHPRA registration in Beresfield?

Visit the Dental Board of Australia at www.dentalboard.gov.au and click "Look up practitioners" to search by practitioner name. All dental practitioners in Australia must hold current registration under the Health Practitioner Regulation National Law. Registration status, conditions, and disciplinary history are publicly searchable via the AHPRA Practitioner Register at practitioners.ahpra.gov.au.
