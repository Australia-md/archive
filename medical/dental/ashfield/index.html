Frequently Asked Questions

How do I verify a dentist's AHPRA registration in Ashfield?

Visit the Dental Board of Australia at www.dentalboard.gov.au and click "Look up practitioners". You can search by practitioner name to view current registration status, any conditions on practice, and disciplinary history. No login is required — the register is publicly accessible.

Which dental clinics in Ashfield are open on weekends?

Several Ashfield clinics offer weekend appointments. Clement Cheung Dental Centre is open Saturday and Sunday 9am–5pm. Pacific Smiles Dental Ashfield is open Saturday–Sunday 9am–6pm. Rumianek Dental Surgery operates Monday–Saturday 9am–8pm. Midway Dental Clinic Ashfield is open Monday–Saturday 7am–7pm. Divine Dental Care (Sat 8am–4pm, Sunday emergencies only), Ashfield Family Dental (Sat 8am–4pm), Dr Ewa Walczak (Sat 8am–4pm), and Simply Braces (Sat 9am–1pm) also offer Saturday sessions. Always confirm current hours directly with the clinic.

Which Ashfield dental clinics offer emergency dental care?

Divine Dental Care sees emergencies on Sundays. Pacific Smiles Dental Ashfield lists daily emergency dental appointments among its services. Midway Dental Clinic Ashfield lists emergency dental services in its offering. Always contact the clinic directly to confirm emergency availability before attending.
