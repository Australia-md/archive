Frequently Asked Questions

How do I verify a dentist's AHPRA registration in Blackburn?

Every practising dentist in Australia must be registered with the Dental Board of Australia. Search the practitioner's name on the Dental Board of Australia site using "Look up practitioners", or on the AHPRA Practitioner Register.

Which Blackburn dental clinics are open on Saturdays?

Of the listed clinics, only Smile To Go publishes hours for every trading day; it opens Saturday 9:00am-1:00pm. Hours for the other clinics were not published for every day, so confirm directly with each practice.

Do any Blackburn dental clinics offer bulk billing?

Healthdirect lists Blackburn Smiles Dental, Hearts Dental and Whitehorse Dental as mixed billing, with bulk billing available with conditions and fees that may apply. Ask the clinic whether you qualify before booking.

Does this page cover Blackburn North and Blackburn South?

No. This page lists clinics with a Blackburn VIC 3130 address. Blackburn North and Blackburn South share postcode 3130 but are listed by Healthdirect as separate localities, and are not covered here.
