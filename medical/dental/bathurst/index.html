Frequently Asked Questions

How do I verify a dentist's AHPRA registration in Bathurst?

Visit the Dental Board of Australia at www.dentalboard.gov.au and click 'Look up practitioners'. Enter the practitioner's name to confirm their current registration status, any conditions on their registration, and disciplinary history. This register is publicly accessible and updated in real time.

Which dental clinics in Bathurst are open on Saturdays?

Bowman Dental (104 Piper Street) is open Saturday 8:00am–12:30pm. Bathurst Dental Care (154 Bentinck Street) is open Saturday 8:00am–4:00pm. Hours sourced from publicly available clinic directories as at 09 June 2026; confirm with the clinic before visiting.

Which dental clinics in Bathurst offer children's dentistry?

Bowman Dental, Centre for Dental Excellence (including CDBS-eligible care for eligible children), and Bathurst Dental Care all list children's dentistry among their publicly confirmed services. Contact each clinic to confirm current availability and any relevant eligibility criteria.

Which dental clinics in Bathurst offer dental implants?

The Dental Practice (90 Keppel Street), Bowman Dental (104 Piper Street — Dr Gerrard Bowman holds a Grad.Dip.Clin.Dent in Oral Implants), and Bathurst Dental Care (154 Bentinck Street) all list dental implants among their publicly confirmed services.
