Dental costs in Australia: CDBS and ADA item numbers

Medicare does not cover most dental care. Children may be covered by the Child Dental Benefits Schedule (CDBS); most adults pay the clinic directly, and private health insurance extras or state public dental services may help. This page does not publish clinic fees — ask Campbelltown clinics for a written quote that lists the item numbers below.

Child Dental Benefits Schedule (CDBS) — national rules from Services Australia, last verified 28 October 2026.
Who can get itChildren aged 0–17 for at least one day of the calendar year who are eligible for Medicare and who, or whose parent, carer or guardian, gets an eligible Services Australia payment at least once that year.
Benefit capUp to $1,158 per child over 2 consecutive calendar years when 2026 is year one ($1,132 when 2025 is year one). Indexed each 1 January.
Coveredcheck-ups, X-rays, cleaning, fissure sealing, fillings, root canals, extractions
Not coveredorthodontic work, cosmetic work, dental services in a hospital
Bulk billingSome dentists bulk bill CDBS services, and then there is nothing to pay or claim. When booking, say you want to use CDBS and ask what each service will cost.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I verify a dentist's AHPRA registration in Campbelltown?

All dental practitioners in Campbelltown must hold current registration with the Dental Board of Australia. Search the register directly at dentalboard.gov.au using the “Look up practitioners” tool to confirm a practitioner's current registration status.

Which dental clinics in Campbelltown are open on weekends?

Several clinics in Campbelltown open on Saturdays, including A Plus Dental, Dental Assembly and Vue Dental Care. Marketfair Dental Care and Pacific Smiles Dental, Campbelltown also open on Sundays, per their stated hours.

Which Campbelltown dental clinics offer emergency dental care?

Gentle Dental Care 7 Days — Campbelltown, Marketfair Dental Care, Pacific Smiles Dental, Campbelltown and Royal Dental Care list emergency dental care among their stated services.

Are there dental clinics near Macarthur Square in Campbelltown?

Macarthur Square Dental — Sydney Smile Care, Macarthur Square Medical & Dental Centre and Pacific Smiles Dental, Campbelltown are all located at or near Macarthur Square Shopping Centre, 200 Gilchrist Drive, Campbelltown.
