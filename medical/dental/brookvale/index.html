Frequently Asked Questions

How do I verify a dentist's AHPRA registration in Brookvale?

Search the practitioner's name on the AHPRA Practitioner Register, or use the "Look up practitioners" tool on the Dental Board of Australia website to confirm current registration status.

Which dental clinics in Brookvale are open on weekends?

Bupa Dental — Warringah Mall and No Gaps Dental Brookvale list Saturday hours; Northern Beaches Family Dental offers Saturday appointments; Pacific Smiles Dental — Brookvale and Primary Dental Brookvale list both Saturday and Sunday hours. Confirm current weekend availability directly with each practice.

Which Brookvale dental clinics offer emergency dental care?

Bupa Dental — Warringah Mall lists dental emergencies, Pacific Smiles Dental — Brookvale lists emergency dental appointments, and Primary Dental Brookvale lists emergency dental care and same-day emergency care in their published services.
