Frequently Asked Questions

How do I verify a dentist's AHPRA registration in Armidale?

All dental practitioners in Australia must hold current registration with the Dental Board of Australia under AHPRA. To verify a practitioner's registration, visit www.dentalboard.gov.au and click "Look up practitioners", then search by the practitioner's name. Registration status, conditions, and disciplinary history are publicly searchable at no cost.

Which dental clinics in Armidale offer emergency dental care?

Based on publicly available information at the time of verification (03 May 2026), Maven Dental Armidale (65 Marsh Street) lists emergency dental appointments among its services, and Markham Street Dental (123 Markham Street) lists emergency dentistry. Contact clinics directly to confirm current availability before attending.
