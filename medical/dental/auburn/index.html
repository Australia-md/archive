Frequently Asked Questions

How do I verify a dentist's AHPRA registration in Auburn?

Visit the Dental Board of Australia at www.dentalboard.gov.au and click "Look up practitioners". Search by practitioner name to confirm current registration status, any conditions imposed on their registration, and disciplinary history.

Which dental clinics in Auburn are open on weekends?

Several Auburn dental clinics operate on Saturdays. Today's Dental Auburn is open Saturday 8am–2:30pm. Master Dental Auburn Shopping Centre is open Monday–Saturday 9am–5pm. My Local Dentists Auburn is open Saturday 9am–1pm. Elite Dental Surgery is open Saturday 9:30am–4pm. Auburn Dental Care is closed on weekends. No clinics listed are open on Sundays. Always confirm hours directly with the clinic before attending.
