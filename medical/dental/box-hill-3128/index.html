Frequently Asked Questions

How do I verify a dentist's AHPRA registration in Box Hill?

Every practising dentist in Australia must be registered with the Dental Board of Australia. Search the practitioner's name on the Dental Board of Australia site using "Look up practitioners", or on the AHPRA Practitioner Register.

Which Box Hill dental clinics are open on Saturdays?

Of the listed clinics with published hours for every trading day, National Dental Care Box Hill opens Saturday 8:00am-5:00pm and My Choice Dental Box Hill opens Saturday 10:00am-3:00pm. Confirm hours for the other clinics directly with each practice.

Do any Box Hill dental clinics offer bulk billing?

Healthdirect lists healthAbility - Box Hill and WYP Dental Clinic - Box Hill as mixed billing, with bulk billing available with conditions and fees that may apply. Ask the clinic whether you qualify before booking.
