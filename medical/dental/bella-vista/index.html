Bella Vista NSW

7 Verified Clinics · Bella Vista · Postcode 2153 · AHPRA Regulated

Bella Vista (postcode 2153) is a mixed residential and business suburb in The Hills Shire, about 33 km north-west of the Sydney CBD. ABS 2021 Census QuickStats records 8,384 residents in Bella Vista, with 82.4% of occupied private dwellings listed as separate houses and 67.3% of residents aged 15 years and over recorded in the labour force. Public sources describe Bella Vista as part of Sydney’s Hills District and the Norwest business precinct, with metro access at Bella Vista station and a concentration of health and commercial services around Lexington Drive, Norbrik Drive and Circa Boulevard. Frequently Asked Questions

How do I verify a dentist’s AHPRA registration in Bella Vista?

Visit the Dental Board of Australia at www.dentalboard.gov.au and click “Look up practitioners” to search by practitioner name. All dental practitioners in Australia must hold current registration under the Health Practitioner Regulation National Law. Registration status, conditions, and disciplinary history are publicly searchable.
