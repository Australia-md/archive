Dental costs in Australia: CDBS and ADA item numbers

Medicare does not cover most dental care. Children may be covered by the Child Dental Benefits Schedule (CDBS); most adults pay the clinic directly, and private health insurance extras or state public dental services may help. This page does not publish clinic fees — ask Avoca Beach clinics for a written quote that lists the item numbers below.

Child Dental Benefits Schedule (CDBS) — national rules from Services Australia, last verified 28 October 2026.
Who can get itChildren aged 0–17 for at least one day of the calendar year who are eligible for Medicare and who, or whose parent, carer or guardian, gets an eligible Services Australia payment at least once that year.
Benefit capUp to $1,158 per child over 2 consecutive calendar years when 2026 is year one ($1,132 when 2025 is year one). Indexed each 1 January.
Coveredcheck-ups, X-rays, cleaning, fissure sealing, fillings, root canals, extractions
Not coveredorthodontic work, cosmetic work, dental services in a hospital
Bulk billingSome dentists bulk bill CDBS services, and then there is nothing to pay or claim. When booking, say you want to use CDBS and ask what each service will cost.
