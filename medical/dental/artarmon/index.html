Frequently Asked Questions

How do I verify a dentist's AHPRA registration in Artarmon?

Visit the Dental Board of Australia at www.dentalboard.gov.au and click "Look up practitioners". Search by the practitioner's name to confirm current registration status, any conditions, and disciplinary history.

Which dental clinics in Artarmon are open on Saturdays?

Both listed clinics have Saturday hours. No Gaps Dental Artarmon (9 Elizabeth Street) is open Saturday 8am–6pm. Artarmon Fine Dental (80 Hampden Road) is open Saturday 8am–2:30pm.

Which Artarmon dental clinics offer emergency dental care?

No Gaps Dental Artarmon and Artarmon Fine Dental both list emergency dental care among their services. Contact the clinic directly to confirm availability for emergency appointments.

Is there a dental clinic near Artarmon railway station?

No Gaps Dental Artarmon at 9 Elizabeth Street is noted as a one-minute walk from Artarmon railway station. Nearby council car parking and limited street parking are also available.
