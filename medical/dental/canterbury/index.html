Which Canterbury dental clinics list parking or wheelchair access?

How do I verify a dentist's AHPRA registration in Canterbury?

Every dental practitioner in Australia must hold current registration with the Dental Board of Australia. Search the practitioner's name on the AHPRA Register of Practitioners, or go to www.dentalboard.gov.au and click "Look up practitioners". The register shows current registration status, conditions and any restrictions.
