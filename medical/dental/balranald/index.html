Frequently Asked Questions

How do I verify a dentist's AHPRA registration in Balranald?

Visit the Dental Board of Australia at www.dentalboard.gov.au and click "Look up practitioners". Enter the practitioner's name to view current registration status, any conditions, and disciplinary history. The register is publicly searchable at no cost.

Which dental clinic in Balranald provides after-hours emergency care?

Balranald Dental Surgery, located within the Balranald Multipurpose Service hospital grounds at 41-43 Market Street, directs after-hours dental emergencies through allied health at Balranald Hospital. Contact the surgery directly on (03) 5071 9808 during operating hours for advice on after-hours access.
