How do I verify a dentist's AHPRA registration in Bangalow?

All dental practitioners in Australia must hold current registration with the Dental Board of Australia. Visit www.dentalboard.gov.au and click "Look up practitioners" to search by name. Registration status, any conditions, and disciplinary history are publicly searchable at no cost.
