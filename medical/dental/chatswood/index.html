How do I verify a dentist's AHPRA registration in Chatswood?

Search the Dental Board of Australia register at www.dentalboard.gov.au ("Look up practitioners") or the AHPRA Register of Practitioners by practitioner name. The register shows current registration status, any conditions, and disciplinary history. Practitioner names for the clinics on this page were not confirmed from public sources at the time of verification, so ask the clinic for the treating dentist's name and check it directly.
