Frequently Asked Questions

How do I verify a dentist's AHPRA registration in Avalon Beach?

Visit the Dental Board of Australia at www.dentalboard.gov.au and click "Look up practitioners". Search by practitioner name to confirm current registration status, any conditions imposed on their registration, and disciplinary history.

Which dental clinics in Avalon Beach are open on weekends?

Avalon Beach Family Dental is open on Saturdays by appointment only. Maven Dental Avalon Beach is closed on both Saturday and Sunday. Neither clinic operates on Sundays. Always confirm current hours directly with the clinic before attending, as appointment-only availability may vary.
