Frequently Asked Questions

How do I verify a dentist's AHPRA registration in Batehaven?

Visit the Dental Board of Australia website at www.dentalboard.gov.au and click "Look up practitioners" to search the public register by practitioner name. All dental practitioners in Australia must hold current registration under the Health Practitioner Regulation National Law. Registration status, conditions, and any disciplinary history are publicly searchable.

What dental services does Batehaven Dental Care offer?
