Key Sectors

Mining & Resources

Australia is the world's largest exporter of iron ore and among the top exporters of coal, LNG, and lithium. The sector is a primary driver of national export revenue.

Agriculture

Australia is one of the few food-surplus nations, with major exports in wool, beef, wheat, and wine.

Financial Services

Sydney serves as the financial hub, dominated by the "Big Four" banks. The superannuation system holds approximately $3.5 trillion in assets.
